Every major exchange that listed KDA announced an exit within about ten weeks of October 2025, each with its own deadline, and every one of those deadlines has now passed. The record below shows what each venue said, when it said it, and what is still worth doing if KDA is sitting somewhere you cannot withdraw from.

Dates are the venues' own. Where a venue gave no deadline, the table says so rather than inventing one.

## You'll see this if

- An exchange emailed you that KDA trading was ending and you never acted on it.
- A wallet balance on an exchange shows KDA but the withdraw button is greyed out.
- You are checking whether a guide you found online is still accurate.
- You are trying to work out whether your old exchange balance is recoverable at all.

Three details are easy to miss and they matter.

**Some venues separated trading from transfers.** OKX suspended deposits first, then buy and sell, then the spot pairs, and kept withdrawals open roughly three months longer. Binance did the same with a two-month gap. If you only read the headline date, you would conclude you had lost access weeks before you actually had.

**Some withdrawals never got a deadline at all.** MEXC said withdrawals would remain available after delisting but set no end date; Binance.US said withdrawals would stay open until further notice. Neither currently publishes a closure announcement that can be verified, which means the answer for those two today is "ask their support", not "it is over".

**Some venues explicitly supported the community chain.** Binance and Gate both issued notices stating they would process withdrawals on the community-maintained network for a window. That was a pragmatic decision, not an endorsement, and it expired on schedule.

## What is still visible in November 2026

A short list, all hedged deliberately because exchange availability changes without notice:

- **Gate** displays a KDA/USDT market. Whether deposits and withdrawals are enabled for your account is something only the asset page in your own account can confirm.
- **XT.COM** moved KDA/USDT to a "hidden as high-risk" status in late October 2026. Hidden is not the same as removed, and it is also not the same as tradable.
- **Flitpay** advertises KDA purchases in INR for Indian users, with identity verification required.
- **Transak** advertises card and fiat purchases of KDA across a set of countries, with availability depending on the checkout.

Nothing in that list is a recommendation, and none of it should be read as evidence that on-chain transfers are open at any of those venues. Check the deposit and withdrawal toggles in your own account before you send anything.

Deliberately absent: Coinbase, Kraken, and Crypto.com. No dated KDA delisting notice from any of them could be verified, so stating one would be inventing a fact. Kraken's KDA *futures* contract settled in March 2025, which is a derivatives event and not a spot delisting.

## If your KDA is still on an exchange

Work in this order.

1. **Log in and open the KDA asset page.** Not the market page. You are looking for the deposit and withdrawal toggles and any banner about network migration or service termination.
2. **Read the venue's own notice, not a news article.** Search their help centre for "KDA" and sort by date. The notice will name the network it supports and any remaining deadline.
3. **If withdrawals are open, move the balance out in one test transaction first.** Send a small amount to an address you control, confirm it lands, then send the rest.
4. **If withdrawals are closed, open a support ticket and ask two specific questions:** whether KDA balances can still be withdrawn manually, and what happens to unconverted balances at account closure. Vague questions get vague answers.
5. **Record everything.** Screenshot the balance, the notices, and your ticket. If a venue later converts balances to another asset, that record is what you will need.

Do not pay anyone who offers to "unlock" exchange-held KDA. Nobody outside the exchange can move a custodial balance, and the offer is always a scam.

## What "converted to stablecoin" really means

Several venues stated that leftover balances might be converted to stablecoins after the deadline, and at least one explicitly said the conversion is not guaranteed. Read that as: the exchange reserves the right to decide later.

If a conversion happens, you have not lost the value outright but you no longer hold KDA, and the exchange decides the rate and the timing. If it does not happen, the balance may sit dormant until account closure. Neither outcome is something you can influence now, which is why the only real window was the one that closed in early 2026.

## The tax question nobody answers for you

Selling at a large loss, or losing access to a balance entirely, has different tax treatment depending on where you live, and in some jurisdictions the two are not treated the same at all. A delisting is not automatically a taxable disposal, and an abandoned balance is not automatically a deductible loss.

What helps, whichever jurisdiction you are in: transaction exports from every venue, dated screenshots of balances and notices, records of what you paid, and a note of when access actually ended. Take those to a local tax professional. No general answer can give you the treatment, and anyone who claims one without knowing your country is guessing.

## Prevention, for the next asset this happens to

- Keep only working balances on exchanges. Custody risk is not hypothetical; it has a deadline attached.
- Treat a delisting announcement as a transfer instruction with a countdown, not as news.
- Follow the venue's help centre rather than social posts, which routinely mix up trading and withdrawal dates.
- Never assume a chain has failed because a venue left. In this case the venues left and the chain stayed.

## FAQ

**Can I still withdraw KDA from Binance?**
Binance's own notice ended KDA withdrawal support on 12 January 2026. Anything beyond that depends on their support team and their leftover-balance policy.

**Was the delisting caused by a chain failure?**
No. Venues cited liquidity, reviews, and risk. Blocks were being produced throughout.

**Is KDA delisted everywhere?**
No. A small number of venues still display KDA markets in November 2026, but the set is unstable and none of them should be assumed to support deposits and withdrawals.

**Could a major exchange relist KDA?**
It is possible and it has happened to other assets, but nothing in the public record suggests it is planned. Do not plan around it.

**Does a delisting change my on-chain KDA?**
No. Exchange listings and on-chain balances are separate. KDA in a wallet you control was never affected.
